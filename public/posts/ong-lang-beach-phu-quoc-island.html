Practical checklist for a day at Ong Lang

Before heading to Ong Lang Beach, Phu Quoc Island, run through a short list:

  • SPF 50+ sunscreen and a hat for 10 AM to 3 PM
  • a beach towel
  • cash for stalls and rentals
  • insect repellent for dusk
  • water, especially if cycling

Good to know

Several mistakes are common. Some visitors expect lifeguards; there are none, so swim close to shore and supervise children. Others swim in rough conditions during the rainy months from July to September without asking vendors about the sea first.

Practical tips

Wi-Fi is available inside some private premises but not on the open beach, so download maps before you go. Restrooms on the sand are limited, so plan to use a restaurant as a paying customer.

Conclusion

Finally, do not leave sunset viewing to the last minute. Arrive 30 minutes early for a good position on the northern rocks. Further reading: a practical checklist for Ong Lang Beach.

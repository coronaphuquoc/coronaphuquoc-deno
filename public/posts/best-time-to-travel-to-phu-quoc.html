An overview of the best time to travel to Phu Quoc

Sitting in the Gulf of Thailand near Cambodia, Phu Quoc has a tropical monsoon climate. Temperatures stay warm through the year, generally from 25C to 30C, so planning a visit here is more about rain and sea conditions than about heat.

Good to know

The calendar splits into two seasons. From October to April, skies tend to be clear, the sea is usually calm and rainfall is low. From May to September, humidity rises, afternoon showers become regular and visitor numbers fall noticeably.

Practical tips

People travel to the island for sandy beaches, coral reefs in the southern waters, jungle trails in Phu Quoc National Park, which covers a large share of the island, and farms producing pepper, honey and fish sauce.

Conclusion

For a classic beach holiday, the dry months are widely recommended. Travellers who prefer quieter roads and fewer crowds may still find the wet months worth a look, as long as their plans can bend around the weather. For more, see the an overview of the Phu Quoc travel seasons.

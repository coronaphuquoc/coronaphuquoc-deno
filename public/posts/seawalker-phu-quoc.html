Reaching the departure point in the south

Getting to this underwater walk means heading to the south of the island. Tours depart from An Thoi Port in An Thoi Ward, Phu Quoc City, An Giang Province, and from there guests take a canoe or speedboat out to the activity platform. The experience itself takes place at Namaste Coral Park, a protected marine sanctuary often near Hon Roi or May Rut Island. Travellers staying up north should factor in the drive down to An Thoi before the boat leg, so leaving with time to spare keeps the morning relaxed. Because the Seawalker Phu Quoc platform is reached by water, your schedule depends on the boat transfer as much as the road trip. Confirm the meeting point and departure time with your operator when you book, since pickup arrangements vary between providers and can change.

Good to know

Hours, prices and conditions change by season, so confirm details locally.

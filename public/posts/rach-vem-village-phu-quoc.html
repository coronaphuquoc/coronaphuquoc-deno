Seafood and Simple Meals at Rach Vem Village Phu Quoc

Eating here revolves around the sea. Meals are built on the day's catch, and the setting is often a floating house on wooden stilts above the water.

Good to know

These "nha be" are homes for fishing families and restaurants at once, with plank floors and metal roofs. Sitting on the planks, guests can watch boats and nets move around them while they eat.

Dishes commonly found on these menus include:

  • Ham Ninh crab
  • herring salad
  • grilled sea urchin
  • grilled shrimp

Practical tips

Minh hoạ: rach vem village phu quoc

For a quicker stop, small eateries on shore serve everyday food such as rice, noodles and pho. These suit travellers who want a light meal between swimming and walking around the village.

Conclusion

Late afternoon is a popular time to eat on the water, since a meal can stretch into sunset. Menus differ from one floating house to the next, so it helps to look at a few before sitting down. For more, see the food and drink near the Rach Vem fishing village.

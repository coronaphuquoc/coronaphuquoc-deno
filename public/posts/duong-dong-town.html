Common Mistakes Visitors Make in Duong Dong Town

Plenty of travellers pass through the island's main centre without really seeing it. A few small adjustments help.

Good to know

Treating it as a transit stop is the first error. Duong Dong Town has temples, markets and factories worth unhurried time, not just banks and rental shops.

🔗 Xem thêm: Eating your way through the Duong Dong night market

Arriving at the evening market at its peak is another. Crowds build from about 6 PM, so turning up slightly before makes browsing and ordering calmer.

Practical tips

Minh hoạ: duong dong town

Sleeping through the morning market means missing a clear view of local life. Activity peaks early, and stalls start closing by mid morning.

Approaching Dinh Cau Temple as a tourist attraction can feel out of place. It is a working shrine, and fishing families pray there, so quiet behaviour matters.

Conclusion

Underestimating travel time from An Thoi Port also catches people out. The road trip to the centre can take 40 to 50 minutes, so allow a buffer before evening plans. For more, see the common mistakes to avoid at Duong Dong town.

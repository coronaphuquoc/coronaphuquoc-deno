Reaching the park entrance

Duong Dong Town is the usual starting point. From there, travel along Nguyen Trung Truc Street towards Bai Thom commune. At the Duong Dong to Bai Thom intersection, turn right and continue for approximately 6 km to the park entrance.

Good to know

From Phu Quoc Airport, head northeast along DT45 for about 35 km.

Transport choices include:

  • Renting a motorbike
  • Renting a car
  • Arranging a driver

Practical tips

Once at the entrance, you park your vehicle and walk approximately 8 km to reach the core rainforest area. That distance is worth planning for, both in time and in water.

Conclusion
